---
name: My Study Helper access requirements
description: Product constraint for future features and content in My Study Helper.
---

My Study Helper must work without paid AI services, API keys, secret keys, or account upgrades. Study explanations and practice content should use built-in sample material.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a working student website without paid APIs, credentials, or account upgrades.

**How to apply:** Keep future study features available without credentials; do not restore API-backed generation as a requirement.
